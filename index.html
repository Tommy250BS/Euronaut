How to play

  • Every daily run has seven questions and no time limit.
  • You get one attempt per question. A wrong answer means no travel, then the next question.
  • Correct answers take you to the country or territory you named.
  • Distance is measured between capital coordinates using great-circle distance.
  • You cannot visit the same destination twice.
  • Badges are awarded for each successful jump; the final score is the sum of kilometres travelled.
